# Review Guidelines

## Core checks

- **Source transparency**: Is the original source clearly cited?
- **Evidence quality**: Is evidence peer-reviewed, preprint, anecdotal, or unclear?
- **Clinical context**: Are limitations and contraindications disclosed?
- **Risk framing**: Are benefits and harms communicated proportionally?
- **Conflicts of interest**: Are funding or affiliations disclosed?

## Output format

For each article, record:
- headline
- publisher
- publication date
- main claims
- supporting evidence
- risk flags
- reviewer notes
- final confidence score
